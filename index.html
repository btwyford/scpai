Bringing People Together Through the Joy of Pickleball

A volunteer-run, not-for-profit association on the NSW Sapphire Coast.

Our Mission

At Sapphire Coast Pickleball Association Inc (SCPAI), our mission is simple: more courts, more players, more smiles.

We believe pickleball is one of the best ways to stay active, meet people, and be part of a vibrant community — and we want as many people as possible on the Sapphire Coast to experience that.

Who We Are

We are a group of volunteers who love pickleball and love the Sapphire Coast.

SCPAI is a not-for-profit association that runs pickleball venues from Bermagui to Pambula. Our sessions are social, supportive, and a lot of fun — everyone from beginners to experienced players is welcome.

Whether you're a retiree looking for low-impact exercise, a competitive player wanting a challenge, or someone who just wants to try something new — you belong here.

Beginners welcome! We provide all equipment and our members love showing newcomers the ropes. Sessions are currently open to players 18 years and over.

About the Committee

The SCPAI committee meets regularly to manage venues, plan sessions, handle memberships, and grow the sport on the Sapphire Coast.

Committee positions are elected at the Annual General Meeting (AGM). All financial members of SCPAI are eligible to vote and stand for election.

We are always looking for enthusiastic members to join the committee — no experience necessary, just a love of the game and the community.
